# Dating Apps with Matchmaker Mode: The New Trend

Remember when your aunt wanted to set you up with "a nice young man"? Well, dating apps just discovered **your aunt was right** 😄.

## The trend: social matchmaking 💘

In September 2026, the app **Rivet** launched nationwide with its "Matchers" feature: anyone can suggest couples among people they know. If both people accept independently, the introduction happens. And successful matchmakers earn **Karma** as a reward.

The idea is simple and powerful: **your friends know you better than any algorithm**. They know who suits you, who makes you laugh, and who doesn't suit you one bit.

## Citas NH already has it 🎉

We're not behind: **Citas NH has its own matchmaker mode**:

1. You **suggest** a couple among people you know.
2. **Each person accepts** (or not) on their own, privately.
3. If both say yes, **the introduction opens up**.
4. You earn **Karma** for every successful match… and even more if the couple ends up chatting!

And the best part: Karma is redeemable for **extra credits and Premium** inside the app. Being Cupid now pays off. 🏹

## Why it works 🧠

- **Less pressure**: being set up by a friend feels more natural than matching with a stranger.
- **More trust**: if your friend recommends them, they come with a reference.
- **More fun**: even people who aren't looking can play matchmaker.

So you know what to do: open Citas NH, put on your Cupid hat, and start uniting hearts. Love (and Karma) await. ❤️
