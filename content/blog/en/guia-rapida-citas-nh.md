# How Citas NH Works: Quick Guide

New here? Welcome to **Citas NH**, the Spanish-first dating app for New Hampshire. This guide gets you up to speed in 2 minutes.

## 1. Create your profile 📝

Sign up with your New Hampshire ZIP, upload your photos, and write a bio that sounds like you. Tip: clear, smiling photos get more likes.

## 2. Verify your selfie 🛡️

So everyone feels safe, selfie verification is **required**. You confirm you're a real person and 18 or older. Without verification, your profile won't show up in matches. That's how we keep fake profiles out.

## 3. Discover and match 💘

Browse profiles of real people in Manchester, Nashua, and all of NH. If you both like each other: it's a match! And the chat opens up.

## 4. Super Likes ⭐

Someone really caught your eye? Send a **Super Like** so they know you mean it. You start with 3 on the house.

## 5. Credits 💳

Viewing photos and chatting uses credits: you get **10 free, refilled every 6 hours**. Want no limits? **Premium** gives you unlimited credits and more perks.

## 6. Matchmaker mode 💘

Got two friends who'd make a great couple? Suggest the match as a **matchmaker**. If both accept, they meet… and you earn **Karma** redeemable for credits and Premium!

## 7. Install it like an app 📲

Citas NH installs straight from your browser — no Play Store or App Store needed. In your profile tap **"Install Citas NH"** and you're done.

Questions? Reach out. And remember: this is about meeting real people, always with respect. ❤️
