# How to Take Profile Photos That Actually Work 📸

On a dating app, your profile photo is your first impression. People decide in **seconds** whether to like you. Here are the secrets of photos that actually work.

## 1. Natural Light, Always ☀️

Daylight is your best filter — and it's free. Step onto the balcony, go to the park, or stand by a window. Avoid night flash: it hardens your features and creates weird shadows.

## 2. Smile for Real 😊

It doesn't have to be a toothpaste-commercial smile. A relaxed, natural smile shows warmth and confidence. Think of something that makes you happy right before the photo.

## 3. Show Your Face 🧑

No sunglasses in every photo, no hats covering everything, no back-turned shots. People want to see who they're talking to. (On Citas NH, verification requires a clear face photo — for a reason.)

## 4. Keep It Decent, Please 🙏

Normal poses, normal clothes, normal backgrounds. Provocative photos **scare away** more than they attract — and on Citas NH they can get your profile rejected during verification.

## 5. Show Your Life 🎣

A photo of you cooking, at the park, with your dog, at a ball game… Photos of you doing things you enjoy give conversation topics and make you look interesting.

## 6. No Group Photos as Your Main 👥

If your first photo has 5 friends in it, nobody knows which one is you. Group shots are fine as photo 3 or 4 — never as the main one.

## 7. Keep Them Updated 🔄

Use photos from this year, not from five years ago. When you meet in person, you want to be recognized — and trusted.

## The Final Trick 💡

Ask a friend to take your photos. Bathroom mirror selfies with a foggy mirror went out of style a long time ago. Good luck — your next match is one good photo away! ❤️
