# 15 Icebreaker Lines 🧊💬

You got the match 🎉. Now comes the million-dollar question: **what do I write?** A plain "hey" isn't enough. Here are 15 lines to start the conversation right.

## Funny 😂

1. "Serious question: does mangú go with los tres golpes, or are you one of the weird ones? 🍳"
2. "Pick one: coffee, a walk, or tacos. Choose wisely 🌮"
3. "Warning: I'm terrible at dominoes, but great company. Deal? 🁫"
4. "What's your useless superpower? Mine is always finding parking 🚗"
5. "If we were a bachata duo, what would our stage name be? 🎸"

## Original ✨

6. "I saw you're into [their interest]. Tell me — how did you get into that?"
7. "Perfect Sunday: adventure or Netflix? There's no wrong answer… almost 😏"
8. "What's the most spontaneous thing you've done this week?"
9. "If you could have dinner with anyone, who would you invite?"
10. "Your photo at [place] looks amazing. How was it?"

## Sweet 💛

11. "I have to admit: your smile won me over before your bio did 😊"
12. "You know what? Of all my matches, your profile made me the most curious."
13. "I promise I won't ask the 'so what do you do?' question… okay, what do you do? 😅"
14. "Coffee this week? No pressure, just good conversation ☕"
15. "I liked your vibe. Want to keep getting to know each other?"

## The Final Tip 💡

The best line is one that **mentions something from their profile**: an interest, a photo, a detail. It shows you actually read it instead of copy-pasting. Good luck! ❤️
