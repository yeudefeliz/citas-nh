# Safety Tips for Your First Date

Meeting someone new is exciting — and with these tips it'll be **stress-free** too. At Citas NH we take your safety seriously: that's why we require selfie verification and 18+ before any profile appears in matches.

## 1. Meet in a public place 📍

A café, a restaurant, a busy park. No going to someone's home on the first date, no matter how nice they seem.

## 2. Tell a friend 📱

Let someone you trust know **where you're going, who you're meeting, and when**. Share your live location if you can.

## 3. Arrive and leave on your own 🚗

Have your own ride or taxi money. That way you can leave whenever you want, without depending on anyone.

## 4. Watch your drink 🥤

Don't accept drinks from strangers and never leave your glass unattended. Golden rule, always.

## 5. Take your time ⏳

No rush to share your address, your exact workplace, or your routines. Trust is earned, not given away.

## 6. Trust your gut 🧠

If something feels off, it feels off. You don't owe anyone an explanation for leaving — just leave.

## 7. Video call first 📹

On Citas NH you can video call before meeting in person. Use it: it's the best way to confirm someone is who they say they are.

## Remember 💡

The vast majority of dates go well and end in laughter. These tips aren't meant to scare you — they're so you can enjoy yourself with peace of mind. Have an amazing date! ❤️
